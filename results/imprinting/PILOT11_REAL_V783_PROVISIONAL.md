## Actual real whole-brain paired experimental results

On the original 159 test-positive source memories, exactly **31** had at least one fourth original literal recall cue that had never been part of Pilot10's three source-authored first/middle/last cue exposures. Using the **same 31 source records and identical frozen synaptic state**:

| Test condition | Correct top-1 | Correct and accepted with previous 0.0082783 threshold | Correct and accepted with validation-only threshold |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Previously trained last cue, paired eligible subset (N=31) | **4/31 (12.90%)** | 4/31 | 0/31 |
| Genuinely unseen fourth literal source cue, same N=31 | **0/31** | 0/31 | 0/31 |
| All original last-trained test cues (N=159) | **20/159 (12.58%)** | 18/159 | 0/159 |

**30/31** of the genuinely untrained fourth cues had **zero shared top-8 active BC01 lexical coordinates with any of their own event's three previously trained source cues**. Even for those 30, the model could emit a nonzero output due to weights written by *other* memories. No genuinely unseen source cue retrieved its own correct event identity in this assay. This is not an independent validated semantic paraphrase test, because the source cues are preexisting recall fields.

## Actual real whole-brain absent-memory rejection test

The original heldout 71 episode-absent test events were never trained, and the acceptance decision is generated by an external oracle scorer, not a neural truth classifier. The original decision threshold was 0.0082783. The new **validation-only** threshold was **0.8043096**, chosen exclusively from 158 non-test positive training records and 62 validation-episode absent queries, with an observed validation false-acceptance cap at 6/62 (9.68%). No original 159 test positive, 71 absent test episode or 31 untrained source-cue result entered calibration.

| Test outcome | Original threshold | Validation-only threshold |
|---|---:|---:|
| Correctly accepted among 159 familiar train-event probes | **18/159 (11.32%)** | **0/159** |
| False acceptance among 71 absent-episode probes | **56/71 (78.87%)** | **5/71 (7.04%)** |
| Accepted at all among 159 familiar train-event probes, correct or incorrect | 142/159 (89.31%) | 18/159 (11.32%) |

This separates a major false-acceptance reduction from a **total loss of correctly accepted familiar-event retrieval** on the heldout set. The gate is not a practical improvement in reliable memory. It cannot identify true autobiography from plausible but incorrect vectors because the lexical distribution and sparse trained state still do not separate them adequately.

## Scientific conclusion and next gate

Original real FlyWire connections can constrain a learned numerical content-specific response for trained BC01 literal cues, but Pilot11 provides no evidence of transfer to genuinely untrained source lexical cues. The original candidate matching is **external oracle-assisted**; no autonomous natural-language memory, semantic understanding, fly physiology, independent character identity or consciousness is established.

The next decisive controls should test a **degree/support/capacity-matched original-biological-connectivity rewired null** and a matched sparse nonbiological network, and separately develop an explicitly versioned cue-semantic generalization method if it can be established without reintroducing an external retriever that dominates the neural substrate. True absent-event rejection must be evaluated jointly with correct-and-accepted rates, never in isolation.
