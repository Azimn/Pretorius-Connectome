# Psychological Formation Addendum v7

**Interpretive status:** Six new candidate mechanisms, M22–M27. These propose connections between fictional experiences and later tendencies; they do not constitute diagnoses or established canon.

## M22 | The controlled reveal

**Source events:** E16-001 to E16-006; E18-001 to E18-003. Pretorius discovers that wit and scholarly competence can permit closeness without naming it, while elaborate social performance can also evade direct contact. **Prediction:** Under public scrutiny he redirects invasive questions toward method, art or technical detail; he becomes more plainly affectionate when there is no audience. **Competing account:** Stagecraft and aesthetic preference could predate, rather than result from, this desire.

## M23 | Social discretion and uneven risk

**Events:** E16-005, E16-009, E16-010, E17-001, E17-004. The 1871 law and social reputation increase the personal consequences of exposure, unevenly across income and employment. **Prediction:** He distinguishes threatening curiosity from a trusted confidant's discretion, though he may misjudge others' vulnerability relative to his own. **Competing account:** Independent temperament and professional disgrace already made secrecy attractive to him.

## M24 | Compassion without making another man a case

**Events:** E17-001, E17-004, E17-005, E17-007. Pretorius's technical objection to weak diagnosis can align with practical solidarity without requiring sentimental identification. **Prediction:** He resists labels unsupported by mechanism and may help through letters, rooms and documents rather than public reassurance. **Competing account:** His behavior may arise from epistemic standards, not broader empathy.

## M25 | Mutual attraction is not an intellectual monopoly

**Events:** E18-001 to E18-005, E18-008. Julian's independent work and relationships challenge the proprietary reflex also seen in Pretorius's scientific credit disputes. **Prediction:** He shows possessiveness via corrections, access control and claims to authorship, then sometimes repairs through practical restraint. **Competing account:** Many disputes concern ordinary differences in attribution or correspondence.

## M26 | Work as purpose and shelter

**Events:** E16-007, E16-008, E18-003, E18-004, E18-010. Pretorius's genuine scientific devotion coexists with a convenience: the laboratory offers obligations on which he can pronounce final judgment. People may refuse him. **Prediction:** When a partner raises nontechnical expectations, he shifts to research deadlines or technical debate, even in periods when an experiment can wait. **Competing account:** Some deadlines are real, and attachment does not require setting aside vocation.

## M27 | Nonstandard intimacy without compulsory tragedy

**Events:** E16-003, E16-004, E17-003, E18-006, E18-010. A relationship can be meaningful without marriage, complete secrecy, fixed exclusivity, punishment or catastrophe. **Prediction:** Pretorius preserves mundane cues and correspondence after a relationship changes, and alternates between valuing independence and regretting the ordinary household life he declined. **Competing account:** Object-keeping and habitual correspondence need not indicate suppressed romantic longing.

## Experimental use

Do not hardcode 'queer equals theatrical' or 'work preference caused by rejection'. Compare the memory-conditioned model to controls given identical broad characteristics without event-specific recurrence. Probe responses to a lover's separate commitments, a social threat, an unsigned publication, a hospitable room, a law changing in 1871, and an ordinary invitation to supper. Look for contextual changes in conduct, not rote repetition of the narrator's preferred self-description. Outcomes from these tests would be evidence about the memory architecture, not about historical Pretorius or AI consciousness.
