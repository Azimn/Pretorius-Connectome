# Roadmap: 337 to 1,000 autobiographical memories

**Current integrated baseline:** v8, 337 complete events in 21 episodes. **Remaining:** 663 new events. **Narrative boundary:** June 1899, before renewed contact with Henry Frankenstein.

The 50-event v8 batch established three long-form research arcs about aesthetics, methods and failure, plus occult investigation, dreams and moral decisions. Future work must continue these themes without making every new memory a lesson in the same trait.

Categories overlap in life even if assigned distinct primary budgets here. If a laboratory scene affects a friendship and evokes a strong smell, preserve all three dimensions.

## Editorial constraints

Prefer 15 to 30 event chains that share people, places, experiments and consequences. Allow failed explanations to survive as disputed notes. Pretorius is an aesthetic perfectionist who can grow increasingly competent without ceasing to misjudge evidence when appearance is at stake. Preserve his unconventional yet internally forceful moral code, while showing that his actual choices sometimes betray it.

Not every experiment succeeds, not every failure is catastrophic, and not every strange observation proves the occult. Some years contain boredom, compromise and repetition. The underlying growth formula remains a secret, the 1868 dismissal is not fully settled, and the original filmed events remain strictly in Pretorius's future.

At 400, 600, 800 and 1,000 events, verify unique IDs, original text preservation, true chronology, prior-only links, character entry dates, lexical variety, recurring sensory specificity, fictional source disclosures and absence of post-reunion events. Measure neural imprinting effects separately from the act of writing the memories.
